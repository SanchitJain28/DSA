# 14-Day Aptitude Roadmap (Placement Prep)

**Time commitment:** 5–6 hrs/day (minimum 3 hrs/day if constrained)

## Daily Time Split
| Block | Duration | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 2 hrs | New topic/concept + untimed practice (20–30 Qs) |
| Afternoon | 1.5–2 hrs | Timed sectional drill on that topic + review every wrong answer |
| Evening | 1–1.5 hrs | Previous day's weak-topic revision + English/vocab drill |

On mock-test days (7, 11, 13): 1 hr full mock + 1.5–2 hrs review. Rest of day light/off.

**Non-negotiable:** never skip review of wrong answers, even if short on time. Cut practice volume before you cut review.

## Day-by-Day Plan

**Day 1–3 — Quant foundation (IndiaBix)**
- D1: Percentages, Profit & Loss, Ratio-Proportion — 20 Qs each, review every wrong answer immediately
- D2: Time-Speed-Distance, Time-Work, Averages
- D3: SI/CI, Mixtures, Number System basics — then one 30-min timed sectional test

**Day 4–5 — Reasoning foundation (IndiaBix)**
- D4: Syllogisms, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding, Direction Sense
- D5: Series, Seating Arrangement, Statement-Assumption/Conclusion — timed sectional at end of day

**Day 6 — English foundation**
- Grammar error-spotting (~20 fixed rules) + 1 RC passage timed. Skip vocab unless target test needs it.

**Day 7 — First full mock (cold)**
- Full-length timed mock (PlacementPreparation.io / Testbook), no prep beforehand
- Rest of day: log every wrong/guessed question by topic → weak-area map

**Day 8–10 — Targeted weak-area drilling**
- Attack only topics that cost marks in Day 7 mock, 1–2/day, timed sectional test each evening
- Time allocation follows the data, not equal split across sections

**Day 11 — Second full mock**
- Compare score/time/accuracy vs Day 7 — this delta is your real progress signal
- Re-log new weak points

**Day 12 — Company-specific pattern**
- Switch to PrepInsta / TCS iON / target company's platform
- Note format quirks: negative marking, sectional cutoffs, time limits

**Day 13 — Third full mock + speed drilling**
- One more full mock under exact exam conditions (same time limit, no pausing)
- Evening: rapid-fire drill on single weakest topic only

**Day 14 — Light revision, no new content**
- Skim your formula sheet / error-spotting rules from Days 1–13
- Do NOT attempt a new mock
- Sleep well

## Tracking
After every mock, log in a simple sheet:
- Accuracy % per section
- Avg time/question per section

This is the only metric that matters — not "did I cover the syllabus."
